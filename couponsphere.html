Good to know

Questions, answered.

How is the 5% commission calculated?

In this prototype, the platform fee is 5% of the coupon's agreed sale price. The checkout preview shows the seller's share and fee separately. You should choose and disclose whether any taxes or gateway fees are additional.

Does the buyer see the seller's QR immediately?

No. The intended flow hides payment details from public listings and from unapproved requests. In production, use a payment provider's checkout or split-settlement product rather than relying on a raw QR to collect and separately recover commission.

Can every coupon be resold?

No. Coupon terms vary. Before launch, verify that the original issuer allows transfer/resale, that the coupon is unused and valid, and that your marketplace rules comply with applicable law and payment-provider policies.

Is this connected to real payments?

No. This is a front-end demo only. It does not accept money, validate coupon codes, send notifications, verify identities, or store listings on a server.
